## How I got here?

I've been seeing an uptick in people writing their own thoughts on the internet
and I have finally broken.
I was following a tutorial about someone setting up their homelab with proxmox and I thought
to myself, 'why can't I do this?'
Not about building the homelab, I think I could do that.
But why can't I create the same content?
Why can't I write up some tutorial and post it somewhere for someone else's search to find?
And then I read an article about [learning in public](https://swyx.io/learn-in-public)
and felt obligated to do this and start sharing more.

## Why am *I* doing this?

I'm usually a private person, so writing my thoughts in a public space
is pretty big for me.
But I've been on a learning grind lately, reading more than usual, and I would like to improve my learning retention.
I find when I read an article, there's a good chance it's going to be forgotten the second
I start on another task.
I hope writing changes that.
Also hoping this helps me not feel overwhelmed by this technology content spike.
Burnout is real.

## What this space will contain?

Since this is a `.dev` domain, it feels right that technical writing would live here.
Hopefully the lifecycle of how my thoughts become a service for me.
Tutorials of technology stitched together.
Recording experiments and writing about new concepts I come across.
Thoughts about the current status of AI and our place in this new era.
I'd really rather not limit myself at such an early time.
We'll see what I become motivated to write about.
Is it wrong for me to write about non-tech things on this site?
Now that I think about it, could I post about sports here?

## What I would like to explore?

New thoughts, new technology, new terms.
Agentic workflows, infrastructure, software engineering all catch my interest at the moment.
I'm interested in processes, project management, team building and other stuff too.
What can I get an AI agent to do for me?

## Conclusion

I wish I had done this sooner.
But better late than never.
Let's see how long I can keep this up.
The next post (or one of the next) will be about how I deploy this blog to the internet.
Stay tuned!
